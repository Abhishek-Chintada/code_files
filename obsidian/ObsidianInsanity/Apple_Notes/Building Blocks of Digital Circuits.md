- [ ] A signal is a variation in the electrical quantity as a function of time, in general the variation is in the current or voltage.
- [ ] If a signal does not change its value with Time then its no longer a signal but called a dc value.
- [ ] A transducer is used to convert the non electrical quantity into an electrical signal.
- [ ] We can define a range for a signal.
- [ ] An analog signal/quantity can take any value at any instant of time within the given range.
- [ ] A discrete time signal is a type of analog signal used to monitor the value of the signal at discrete points/interval in time.
- [ ] The actual use of a signal is when we actually store it or transmit it.
- [ ] Similar to how we have discretised the time in the discrete time signal, we can also discretise the amplitude. When we discretise both the time and amplitude, we get a DIGITAL SIGNAL.
- [ ] We can only store or transmit the signal at discrete levels. The accuracy of the usage depends on the number of levels we have used.
- [ ] We can also represent the levels with a range.
- [ ] Lower number of levels allow us to easily transmit the signal. Higher number of levels are used when accuracy is required.
- [ ] The above flexibility is available in digital but not in the analog methods.
- [ ] Analog signals can also be upgraded by using better software whereas the digital signals can be upgraded by using more levels.
- [ ] Reverse transducer is used to convert the digital signal to analog.
WHY DIGITAL ?
- [ ] Digital signals are much easy to store and manipulate as compared to the analog signals.
- [ ] The effect of noise on a digital transmission is much lesser than the effect of noise on an analog transmission.
- [ ] We can easily increase the accuracy of the digital signal as compared to the analog signal.
- [ ] The budget for the same extent of upgradation of digital is much less than that of the analog.
- [ ] So we can safely say that the digital signals are more reliable than the analog signals.
WHY DO WE NEED ANALOG ?
- [ ] All real life signals are analog signals.
- [ ] It is almost impossible to get away with the analog signals.

- [ ] We take an analog signal and pre process it if necessary, convert it into digital, process the digital signal, convert it back to analog and give the output.
- [ ] We study about the analysis, usage, and design of the digital systems and circuits.
- [ ] There are two types of digital circuits which are COMBINATIONAL and SEQUENTIAL.
