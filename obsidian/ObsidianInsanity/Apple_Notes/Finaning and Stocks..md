While investing one may fall into the following asset classes :
1. Fixed income instruments.
2. Equity.
3. Real estate.
4. commodities(precious metals)

Fixed income instruments : these are the investment avenues where the principal amount or in simple terms the money that we invest is perceived to be safe. The banks fixed deposit schemes are one example of this kind. Few other examples include :
1. Bonds issued by the Government of India (also called G Sec bonds and T Bills)
2. Bonds issued by Government related agencies such as GAIL, HUDCO, NHAI, etc
3. Bonds issued by corporate’s (Tata, Bajaj, Reliance, Adani)

Equity : these type of investments are based on buying shares of publicly listed companies. These stocks are exchanged on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE). Unlike the fixed income instrument these do not provide a capital guarantee but as a trade off offer better incomes of used wisely. Investing on the best and well run companies in India can be very yielding but that requires a lot of skill, hard work and patience.
